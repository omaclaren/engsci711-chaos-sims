iPad-friendly, self-contained simulations: Lorenz, sensitive dependence, Lorenz map, logistic/tent maps.
Lorenz system
x' = σ(y−x)
y' = x(ρ−z)−y
z' = xy−βz
Teaching point: no stable fixed point/periodic orbit in this parameter range, but trajectories remain bounded and approach a strange attractor.

Rössler system
x' = −y−z
y' = x + ay
z' = b + z(x−c)
Teaching point: another simple 3D ODE with a strange attractor; often visually simpler than Lorenz.
